# Novelty and claim boundary

## What is already known

Correlated signed position-decoding errors are not a new general concept.

- Saleem et al. showed coherent CA1--V1 position errors and linked them to licking
  behavior in virtual reality: <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0516-1>.
- Tang et al. showed coherent CA1--PFC position errors on a theta-cycle timescale:
  <https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2996-18.2019>.
- van der Goes et al. studied coordinated decoded head-direction errors in ADN and
  RSC. Its peer review is especially relevant because it raised the common-tracking
  and decoder-reliability artifact directly:
  <https://elifesciences.org/articles/82952> and
  <https://elifesciences.org/articles/82952/peer-reviews>.
- Gonzalez et al. established CA3--CA1--RSC subspace structure, lead--lag relations,
  and spatially structured members in the source recordings, but did not test
  within-traversal correlations of independently decoded position deviations:
  <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10481-z>.

## Candidate contribution

If the sealed analysis confirms, the new result is regional and conditional:

> Within-traversal signed deviations of independently decoded position covary
> between CA1 and deep-layer RSC, and the relation is not fully accounted for by
> measured position, nonlinear speed, population spike totals, trial order, or the
> independently decoded CA3 deviation in these recordings.

The CA3 statement requires its own held-out control to pass; it is not inherited
from a positive primary result.

## Claims the data cannot support

- subjective position, because the released NWB files do not contain licking,
  reward-choice, or navigation-error timestamps that independently report the
  animal's estimate;
- causal or directed CA1-to-RSC communication;
- a new general principle of coherent spatial coding;
- independence from all behavior (only position and speed are measured);
- replication in new animals, because the sealed fixed-condition sessions come
  from three mice already represented in discovery;
- generality across tasks, environments, sexes, or RSC layers.

The strongest accurate description is a held-out-session replication within the
recorded animals.
